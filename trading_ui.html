Collection health

Paper collection runs in the local server's background collector. It does not need this page, a browser or an AI session. Closing every dashboard page does not stop it. The computer must be switched on, awake and online, and the server process must be running. The Windows logon task starts it only while you are signed in; a locked screen is fine. Sleep, hibernation, shutdown, signing out or a network outage stop collection. Bars missed during that time are never backfilled and remain visible as gaps. There is no cloud copy.

Checking the background collector…

A check is a provider request. A new observation is a bar the paper books had not recorded before; re-checking a bar that is already recorded is not new. Market closed means no bar is due under the bundled NYSE 2026–2027 calendar. Crypto trades around the clock. A running server, or a Pause button showing it is unpaused, does not by itself prove collection; use the latest new observation times.

Checking saved records…

Record age warnings use 4 days for daily records and 30 minutes for the active experiment. These are display reminders, not market-calendar checks. A recent observation is not proof of gap-free collection. Balances are valued at the last recorded prices. No missed bars are replayed as forward trades.

SIX ACCOUNTS · $25,000 EACH · PAPER ONLY

Stock strategy experiments

Trend, breakout and mean reversion each run on 5-minute and 15-minute bars. Each account keeps its own balance and history. Equal-weight buy-and-hold reference books use the same prices and modeled trading costs. Reference capital is comparison money, not an extra allocation to the strategies.

The background collector checks each cadence shortly after every bar completes during regular NYSE sessions. It retries at most once a minute and does not need this page to be open. Signals use completed bars and may fill only on a later observed bar. A 5-minute rule can therefore enter or exit within the day, but does not promise a trade every five minutes. Missing or stale prices hold the account. Long-only, no leverage; unused money stays in cash. Positions can remain open overnight.

More parallel experiments provide comparisons sooner, not independent market history or reliable profit projections. Costs are modeled; these are not broker fills or subsecond trading. Existing core and crypto accounts retain their balances.
